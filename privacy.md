# Privacy Policy

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner for personal rclone backups of their own Google Drive.

The application may access files, folders, file metadata and authentication tokens required to perform backup operations.

No user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics.

All access and processing occurs only between the owner's computer and Google Drive through the Google Drive API.

Authentication credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not shared with third parties.
